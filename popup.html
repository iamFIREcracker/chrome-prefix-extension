Color mode: press 1–8 or click a color.

1–8 color the banners, Alt+1–8 (or Alt+click) the page; 0 / Alt+0 remove them.

Other shortcuts

  • Alt+Ccolor mode, then 0–8 or Alt+0–8
  • ( )previous / next tab
  • cnew tab
  • &close tab
  • rreload extension
  • zkeep screen awake (toggle)
